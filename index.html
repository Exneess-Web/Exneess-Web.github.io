Understanding Spreads, Commissions and Leverage

A spread is the difference between the buy and sell price quoted for an instrument. Some account types primarily use spread-based pricing, while others may combine tighter spreads with a separate commission. Actual costs can vary with market conditions, instrument, account and execution.

Leverage allows a trader to control a larger market position relative to the margin deposited. It can increase both potential gains and potential losses. The leverage available may depend on the instrument, account, client classification and regional rules. Always review the current product specifications and trading conditions before making a decision.
